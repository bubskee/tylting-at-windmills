How the letters work

Each letter measures how many of the baseline’s misses the technique recovers: (hits − baseline hits) / (trials − baseline hits). S starts at 90%, A at 65%, B at 35%, C above zero. Matching or trailing the baseline earns D. If the baseline is perfect, the trial has no grade.
