Who Let the Mouse Peek?

Can the model change where the mouse looks while remembering where the biscuit is?

You saw the owl move the biscuit. Did the mouse? A peephole can change where she’ll look first even though the biscuit ends up in the same tin. We borrowed the logic of the unexpected-transfer task so the model could get the world right and the mouse wrong in the very same story.

The biscuit moves in every story. Opening the peephole changes what the mouse can see. Both questions read the same story up to the patch at its final period. The reference stories cross visibility with the starting color.
