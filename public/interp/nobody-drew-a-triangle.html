Nobody Drew a Triangle

Can a patch of empty paper tell us whether the model sees a triangle?

Look between the three disks. You can see an edge there without anyone drawing a line. Turn the mouths outward and the middle of the image is still the very same white square. We copied the model’s state for that square to ask how much news from the surrounding disks had reached it.

Illusion behind our experimentPerceptual Grouping without Awareness · Kanizsa triangle and rotated controlZoo experiment · saved rows

Both pictures are real image inputs. At the middle of each, the same 32 × 32 square is pure white. The patch copies the corresponding image-token state at the center of the 9 × 9 grid in the language decoder, after the vision encoder has processed the whole picture. Eight other rotations and placements supplied the fit.

The logit margin compares the two printed answer tokens. “Top token” includes the entire vocabulary. The model uses checkpoint c202236.
