Cue the Understudy

Silence the lead heads

What does every other tool say?

Alice and Bob arrive at the library. Alice hands a book to … You can probably finish the sentence. In GPT-2 small, some heads help put the recipient’s name on the page. Silence the usual heads and others pick up the work. A small drop in accuracy can hide a busy understudy.

Original studyWang et al. · Backup name movers, §3.4Zoo experiment · saved rows

Published experiment. In GPT-2 small, silencing the three primary heads that copied a recipient’s name reduced the correct name’s average lead by only 5%. Other heads took on more of the work. Indirect object identification, §3.4 ↗

Proposed range test. Predict the effect of silencing the leads, the backups, and both together before revealing any of the three. Repeat with fresh names and matched control heads. Proposed readout. follow an understudy’s contribution to each name while the leads are still onstage, then take them away.
