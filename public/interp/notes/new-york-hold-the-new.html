A traveler from Manhattan and a traveler from Newark can both start the name of their state with “New.” Their next words part company. A reader that can already distinguish York from Jersey might be picking up a plan one word before you can see it.

Original studyPal et al. · Future LensZoo experiment · saved rows

Published experiment. Future Lens transplants one GPT-J hidden state into a different context to recover information about upcoming tokens. Its Madison Square Garden example illustrates how the state that predicts New can also carry evidence about York. Future Lens ↗

Proposed range test. Find source prompts the clean model completes with New York and New Jersey. Transplant the state from before New into the same receiver, then supply New in both trials. Does the next token follow the donor? Check the receiver’s preference without a transplant. Proposed readout. see whether a plain logit lens on the donor state already reveals York or Jersey.
