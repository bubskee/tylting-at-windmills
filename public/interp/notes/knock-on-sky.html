Knock on Sky

Put a door in the sky

What does every other tool say?

A church wall and a patch of sky get the same door brush. The wall grows an arched opening. The sky gets a little gray mark. Once you’ve seen both pictures, “door unit” starts to sound like a question about what the rest of the scene will allow.

Original figureBau et al. · GAN Dissection, Fig. 11Zoo experiment · saved rows

Published experiment. In a church-scene image generator, activating twenty door units can put a door in a wall. The same intervention in the sky or a tree usually fails. You can see the dependence on the surroundings in one picture. GAN Dissection, Figure 11 ↗

Proposed range test. Click a window, a bare wall, and a patch of sky. Give the original units and a PCA direction fitted to door and wall patches the same placements. Predict where each door will appear and what else will change in the image. Proposed readout. follow later activation maps at the spot where you asked for a door and got only sky.
