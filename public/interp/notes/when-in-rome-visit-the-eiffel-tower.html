Suppose a fictional guidebook puts the Eiffel Tower in Rome. You want the model to follow the guidebook, and you still want it to know the capital of France. Ask both questions after the weight edit. That second question puts a price on moving the tower.

Original demonstrationMeng et al. · ROME and the Eiffel TowerZoo experiment · saved rows

Published experiment. ROME changes weights in one GPT-J MLP so the model places the Eiffel Tower in Rome. The published change carried into travel questions and suggestions about nearby sights. ROME demonstration ↗

Proposed range test. Ask a temporary activation edit to produce the same change. Ask where to visit the tower, then where to visit the Louvre. Record each time the temporary edit has to be reapplied. Proposed readout. overlay both landmarks on a PCA map fitted to clean landmark prompts. Does one move, or do half the sights of Paris come along?
