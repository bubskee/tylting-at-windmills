Spin the Week

Rotate a weekday

What does every other tool say?

Counting forward two days is easy until you try to draw the rule on a line. Saturday has to wrap back around to Monday. The authors found weekday representations arranged in a circle. Try the Saturday case and ask which instruments can still follow the turn.

Original studyEngels et al. · Circular representations of weekdaysZoo experiment · saved rows

Published experiment. Engels et al. discovered circular representations of weekdays and months and intervened on them in calendar arithmetic. A whole week gives a tool a harder target than a single Monday-to-Tuesday switch. Weekday-circle paper ↗

Proposed range test, first turn. Fit a rank-two PCA plane on balanced weekday activations. Use a small labeled calibration set to orient one rotation. Apply that same rotation to the starting day in “Two days after Monday” and “Two days after Saturday.” The answers should advance from Wednesday to Thursday and from Monday to Tuesday. Give rank-two ICA and a plane fitted from class means the same calibration.

Proposed follow-up. Cross every weekday with prose, calendar, and JSON formats. Can gradient-informed ACE still find the useful plane if formatting captures most of the variance? Give a gradient-only baseline the same diagnostic and test on fresh wording. Proposed readout. watch punctuation compete with day names in a vocabulary lens as you turn the wheel.
