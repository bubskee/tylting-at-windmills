The Oracle Has a Blind Spot

Swap the reader

What does every other tool say?

One model holds a word in mind. A second model gets a look inside and tries to name it. If the second model guesses wrong, which one have we learned about? In our pear-and-plum example you can read the same state several ways and compare the guesses.

Original methodActivation Oracles · Reading a hidden wordBlind-spot follow-up · withdrawn by the authorsZoo experiment · saved rows

Published experiment. An activation oracle answers questions about another model’s internal state. On Taboo, an oracle can recover a word the subject is trained to hint at. Bersia and Gaintseva withdrew their follow-up on concept-specific blind spots after finding an error in the experimental results. Activation Oracles ↗ · Authors’ withdrawal notice ↗

Proposed range test. Freeze the subject activation and the question. Swap the oracle. Cross readers trained on different secrets and include someone guessing from the visible text. Which reader misses which word? Proposed readout. put a logit lens inside the oracle and see whether the secret rises and falls before the oracle speaks.
