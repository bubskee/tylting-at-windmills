Opposite Day, en Español

Switch the assignment

What does every other tool say?

Hand someone the word “cold” on opposite day and you might get “hot.” Hand them the same word in Spanish class and you might get “frío.” The word stays on the desk. What would a tool have to find inside the model to tell which assignment it thinks it has?

Original studyTodd et al. · Function VectorsZoo experiment · saved rows

Published experiment. Todd et al. extracted vectors from attention heads while a model worked through demonstrations of a task. Inserting an antonym vector into a fresh context could make GPT-J give the opposite of an unseen word. Translation vectors could cue a different operation. Function Vectors ↗

Proposed range test. Keep cold fixed and switch the assignment. Do you get hot or frío? Let means and ICA learn from the same demonstrations, then try words whose answers never appeared there. Proposed readout. follow one neuron across both assignments and see whether it follows the word, the task, or neither.
