Made You Blink

Erase a lens direction

What does every other tool say?

Imagine watching a lens’s prediction of “red” disappear as you erase a direction. Has the model changed its mind too? The tuned-lens paper checked both ends of that experiment. Our color prompt gives you a place to put an intermediate reading next to the answer the model actually gives.

Original figureBelrose et al. · Tuned Lens, Fig. 8Zoo experiment · saved rows

Published experiment. Belrose et al. erased directions that affected a tuned lens and checked the final model output. At layer 18 of Pythia-410M, the rank correlation between a direction’s influence on the lens and on the model was 0.89. This result ranks how much the distributions moved. An individual token’s fate is a separate question. Tuned Lens, Figure 8 ↗

Proposed range test. Pick two directions. Bet on which erasure moves the model more, then name one token that will gain probability. Score the two bets separately. Proposed readout. give a raw logit lens the same edited activations and open a prompt where the readers disagree.
