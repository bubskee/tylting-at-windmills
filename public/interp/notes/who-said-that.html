Who Said That?!

Trace the copied token

What does every other tool say?

Read “A B C B A” and pause. If you expect B next, the first A has just given you a clue. Change its neighbor to D and leave the final A in place. A head that looks back at the right letter is a promising suspect. The next question is whether it helps deliver the answer.

Original studyOlsson et al. · In-context Learning and Induction HeadsZoo experiment · saved rows

Published experiment. Induction heads can promote the token that followed an earlier occurrence of the current token. Olsson et al. tested their causal contribution with head ablations in small attention-only models. Induction heads and direct ablations ↗

Proposed range test. Start with A B … C B … A and A B … C B … C. Both suggest B. Replace only the first B with D. Which prediction follows? Use random single-token symbols and measure the odds again with the candidate head silenced. A lens can call both original answers correctly and still leave you guessing which B to change. Proposed readout. inspect both earlier B positions with the same logit lens.
