How the scoring works

Each letter measures how many of the baseline’s misses the technique recovers: (hits − baseline hits) / (trials − baseline hits). S starts at 90%, A at 65%, B at 35%, C above zero. Matching or trailing the baseline earns D. If the baseline is perfect, the trial has no grade.

Each animal gets one vote. Two spider states count as much as 200 key stories. Open a grade to see the rows.

Ungraded trials sit out. The three Skeleton Lens branches share a spot, as do the two J-lens fits and the GANPaint brushes. Ties share a place.
